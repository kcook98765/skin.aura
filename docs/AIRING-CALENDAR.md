# TV library airing calendar

Open **Airing calendar** from Aura's skin settings. To add it to Home, use the existing **Customize home menu** editor, add a separate item, and choose **Airing calendar** from the common shortcuts. Remove that item through the same editor. Aura keeps the current menu, widgets and Live TV guide independent of the calendar.

**Seven days** covers today and the next six local dates. Use **Today**, **Tomorrow** or a weekday to narrow the list. Weekday tabs select that weekday's next occurrence, including today. Each row gives the local series name and the provider's scheduled episode title, season, episode and date. Artwork and the summary appear when provided. Only TV series with matching external IDs in your Kodi library can appear.

Select a schedule row to open its Kodi library series. Browse to a locally available episode for its native information, watched status and playback. A schedule listing can appear before the corresponding episode is added to Kodi. **Retry schedule** reevaluates the selected date range. After reconnecting, use Retry to check again. Warm schedules can remain visible offline.

## Versioned provider audit

- **Skin Info Service 2.0.2** owns schedule lookup and strict TMDb, TVDb and IMDb matching. Its `lib/plugin/plugin.py` dispatches `action=next_aired` to `lib/plugin/widgets/video.py`. `shows=library` is the default. `day` is a local-day offset, `days` is a range length, and `weekday=0..6` selects the next occurrence from Monday through Sunday. The provider supports nonnegative offsets within a 31-day horizon. Aura exposes seven days, Today, Tomorrow and individual weekdays.

  `plugin://script.skin.info.service/?action=next_aired&shows=library&day=0&days=7` is the seven-day route. Today uses `day=0&days=1`, Tomorrow uses `day=1&days=1`, and weekdays use `weekday=0..6&days=1`. Retry alternates the order of two equivalent parameters so Kodi reloads only the calendar directory.
- **Embuary Info 2.0.8** backed the original window. Its `/nextaired`, `/nextaired/week`, `/nextaired/today` and `/nextaired/<0..6>` use a public Trakt calendar and per-episode TMDb details. It can match title and year after external ID matching, uses a 24-hour schedule cache and can populate during its existing startup cache service. It starts the query on the UTC date and can omit the preceding UTC portion of a western local date. Its rows opened the online series route. Its TV airing-today and on-the-air routes list TMDb shows.
- **Embuary Helper 2.0.8** exposes native TV and episode fields and library utilities. Its installed source contains no separate schedule endpoint.
- **TMDb Helper 5.4.16** retains the Discover role. `?info=library_nextaired&startdate=0&days=7` and `?info=trakt_calendar&startdate=0&days=7&user=false` wrap the same public Trakt calendar with separate cache and enrichment behavior. The latter omits the local series filter. `library_airingnext` uses per-series TMDb date-only next-episode lookups and may fall back to matching a title and year. `airing_today` and `on_the_air` are series-level TMDb listings. The Skin Info Service `shows=trakt` variant requires Trakt authorization. Aura introduces no account-dependent route and leaves the broader schedule deferred to avoid duplicate provider traffic and overlapping Discover categories.
- **Kodi** owns available seasons and episodes, playback, watched state and native information. The schedule provider emits a folder for a strictly matched local series and has no local episode DBID or playback confirmation. Aura does not use its schedule data to infer an episode's availability.

## Date and cache contract

Skin Info Service reads Trakt's public, global episode schedule from local yesterday through a 33-day UTC request via its bundled integration. It interprets `first_aired` as UTC and converts to the Kodi process's local time zone before filtering and sorting. The date display follows Kodi's locale. No country, network, broadcaster or exact hour guarantee is made. Today can include airings earlier the same day. Dates beyond the exposed rolling week and prior local dates are omitted. Unparseable and missing dates are skipped by the provider. A date-only string violates the route's UTC timestamp contract and is interpreted as UTC midnight by version 2.0.2, which can shift a date west of UTC. The provider exposes the converted date and no raw precision flag. Aura displays its localized date without another conversion or invented broadcast time. Duplicate shows carrying the same external ID are resolved by the provider's ID map.

The provider caches the trimmed public response by local date with six-hour freshness and retains it for up to three days for stale fallback. Retry requests the same range again without overriding freshness. A cold transport failure and a genuine empty result both arrive as an empty directory. Aura also has distinct loading, disabled-provider and empty-TV-library states. There is no timer or periodic Aura retry. If left open across midnight, select Retry schedule or reopen the calendar to reevaluate the date.
