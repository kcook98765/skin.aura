2026-11-14 ALPHA DISTRIBUTION SUCCESSOR — DISPOSITION

The preceding findings describe the inherited baseline and remain for historical
notice preservation. This successor changes the following current payload:

- Removed SF Distant Galaxy, Nasalization, Geomancy Extra Bold, BOXING and
  Theboldfont binaries. Font.xml retains lyric role names but uses existing
  Exo 2, Poller One and Ranchers instead. Original supplied license documents,
  including the Nasalization PDF, remain verbatim; they do not cover substitutes.
- Removed all 483 Knavs language flag PNGs. Audio/subtitle rows now display actual
  language codes with the normal skin font. Exact legacy metadata is preserved
  in resources/LEGACY-ASSET-NOTICES.txt; no new flag license is asserted.
- Replaced ten photographic album fallbacks with procedural geometric artwork;
  omitted two unused photographic default icons, eleven commercial-art view
  previews and five inherited screenshots. No replacement screenshot is claimed.
- New icon/fanart and album fallbacks are reproducible with
  tools/prepare_alpha_artwork.py in the source checkout. These simple geometric
  and typographic compositions are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, like Aura; they are not native
  captures. Former Matrix title artwork contributor: Muhammad Hussein Ammari,
  2022 (retained history commits cc4825a and 5e0ebb9).
- Retained Apache/OFL fonts contain embedded copyright/license statements.
  Full Apache text is additionally supplied as fonts/Apache-2.0.txt and the
  LineSpacingMod change notice as fonts/Roboto-Regular-LineSpacingMod-NOTICE.txt.
  Incomplete byte-build tracing alone is not treated as evidence of prohibition.
- iconmonstr / Alexander Kahlkopf icons remain solely within Aura's application
  design, relying on the application-design permission in section 2 of its
  separate terms. Full current terms: resources/ICONMONSTR-LICENSE.txt. These
  icons are not relicensed under Aura's CC license, and this distribution grants
  no standalone icon-library redistribution or extraction rights. Brand symbols
  identify UI formats/services; no affiliation, endorsement or trademark license
  is asserted. Any use outside this application context needs its own rights.
- Generic inherited Aura geometry/textures remain under the upstream project
  license with jurialmunkey attribution. This is a bounded application-context
  review, not a warranty of all historical third-party ownership or legal advice.
